A software experiment cannot qualify a production profile or physical clock.
Final production calibration and physical measurements remain outside this work.
An unavailable executable producer makes a six-producer measurement inconclusive;
it cannot be reported as zero occupancy. A missing hardware origin, frame
alignment, validity or error bound makes a timing report inconclusive or stopped
under the existing observability law.

## Implementation order

Prepare the exact backend proposal while designing the observer ownership lane.
Build and verify the observer lane in the application layer, which already owns
a bounded SPSC dependency. Then extend logical matching and the mixed owner as
one identity-preservation design before connecting application control and input.
Exercise remaining producer paths only through their real first consumers.
The final audit revisits all six rows, their code, tests and outstanding gates;
committing one slice does not complete another row.
